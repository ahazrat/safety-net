### Community-Based Safety Network - Example

- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/gxzYUzxI)

[https://www.reddit.com/r/GoldandBlack/comments/tas9av/people\_in\_minneapolis\_are\_calling\_each\_other/](https://www.reddit.com/r/GoldandBlack/comments/tas9av/people_in_minneapolis_are_calling_each_other/ "‌")

Each street has a captain. This captains belong to a larger group who pass information within that area

civilian patrollers who use green lights and marked vehicles

captain's are in communication with local security as well as the local law enforcement

Any patrollers answer to their particular area captain, who also puts together their rosters for patrols

Guys also have radios to call in anything suspicious. But they are not allowed to directly engage unless absolutely necessary.

In most cases it's about getting a first responder on scene and waiting for actual law enforcement to handle it if possible.

In most cases of petty theft, the guys are able to apprehend the suspect and wait on scene.

Members do also have very strict rules to avoid potentially causing damage or commuting a felony.

The goal is to create enough presence and awareness of what is happening. This deters most criminals. So we only see the occasional chancer, or professionals.

A few weeks ago there was a robbery and murder in broad daylight, which is unusual. But the vehicle had already been identified as suspicious before it happened.

So people were in scene almost immediately, and they were able to continue tracking the suspects. We also have what's called sniper cams setup at every main entry/exit of our area.

The patrol group captains and local law have access to them, and can log or search for specific vehicles by their plates.

organizing with your neighbors, knowing who they are, interacting with them, being able to recognize all of them, being able to identify them all on sight, and being able to identify people who don't belong are great steps towards protecting your neighborhood

That all requires things like taking the responsibility to talk to the people around you and learning how to be social so you can figure this shit out.
