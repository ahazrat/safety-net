# How to Make an App

10 cards.

### 1. Idea and Research

- labels: Complete
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/jzg6hHmo)

- Start by brainstorming app ideas. Think about the problem your app will solve or the value it will provide to users.
- Conduct market research to see if there's a demand for your app and if similar apps already exist. Understanding your target audience and competitors is crucial.

The idea of the app and the problem it will solve is resolving in the General Notes list.


### 2. Define the Scope

- labels: Complete
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/KdnEMIq3)

- Clearly define what your app will do and what features it will include. Create a list of essential features for the initial version (MVP - Minimum Viable Product).


### 3. Design and Prototyping

- labels: Complete
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/hamRdiMQ)

- Create wireframes and mockups of your app's user interface to visualize its layout and flow. You can use design tools like Sketch, Figma, or Adobe XD.
- Develop a clickable prototype to simulate how the app will work and gather feedback from potential users.


### 4. Choose a Platform

- labels: Complete
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/rCyYFpLz)

- Decide whether your app will be for iOS, Android, or both (cross-platform). You can develop natively for each platform or use cross-platform development frameworks like React Native or Flutter.

Decision: The app will be for both iOS and Android (**cross-platform**).

Decision: The development framework will be **React Native**

Explore: What are the pros/cons of Flutter (compared with RN)


### 5. Development

- labels: In Progress
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/rh4HBzvu)

- Start coding the app based on the design and prototype. Break down the development process into smaller tasks to make it more manageable.
- Integrate necessary APIs and backend services if your app requires server-side functionality.


### 6. Testing and Debugging

- labels: In Progress
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/bBH0D81Z)

- Perform thorough testing to identify and fix any bugs or issues.
- Conduct usability testing with real users to gather feedback and make necessary improvements.


### 7. Polish and Refinement

- labels: In Progress
- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/SSklxsRq)

Enhance the user experience, fine-tune the app's performance, and address any user feedback.


### 8. App Store Submission

- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/V7Twethd)

- Prepare all necessary app assets and information, such as descriptions, screenshots, and icons.
- Submit your app to the respective app stores (Apple App Store for iOS apps and Google Play Store for Android apps).


### 9. Launch and Marketing

- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/siS6dK2h)

- Plan a marketing strategy to promote your app and increase its visibility.
- Utilize social media, app review websites, and other online channels to reach your target audience.


### 10. Gather Feedback and Iterate

- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/SYx9XfTG)

- Monitor user feedback and analytics to identify areas for improvement.
- Continue iterating and updating your app to provide new features and enhancements.
