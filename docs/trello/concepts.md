### Location

- [Trello card](https://trello.com/c/XdzdbSsD)

Location is determined by a hierarchal, geo-spatially defined region in space.

1. Planet
2. Continent
3. Country
4. State
5. County
6. Town / City
7. Street Name
8. Street Number
   1. Parcel Number
9. Unit Number
10. Zoning

A point is a pair of GPS coordinates relating position by value of:

- Latitude (horizontal lines, lat)
- Longitude (vertical lines, long)

The lat specifies the north–south position of a point on the surface from the equator. The long specifies the east–west position of a point on the surface from the prime meridian.

The unique geo-spatial signature of a location can be identified with a GPS-indexed boundary.
