# One Chicago neighborhood roster

Ops checklist for a single watch or business corridor. Not a national import, not a citywide scrape, and not a faker seed on the public map.

Live app: https://safetynet-us.web.app

## 1. Pick the place

Name one geography you can walk: one community area, one corridor, or one existing watch. Write the streets and the group in one sentence. Stop there. Do not add a second neighborhood in this pass.

## 2. Outreach (utility first)

Say this, in this order:

> Hire a neighbor to watch the block. See jobs and risk on a map. Private contracts. Public map.

Link https://safetynet-us.web.app/map and https://safetynet-us.web.app/services (Neighborhood Watch, Event Security, Physical Defense).

Providers are independent contractors. Badges are admin-attested, not licenses. Do not say SafetyNet replaces 911, police, or dispatch. Do not lead with philosophy.

## 3. Onboard 5–10 providers

- [ ] Each person creates their own account on `safetynet-us`. They choose the password. No shared demo login.
- [ ] Each sets a public username. Email stays on `users/{uid}`.
- [ ] An existing admin grants badges by hand on Admin. At least one badge each. Do not market weapons-lethal matching.
- [ ] Count is 5–10 people in this one corridor, not a metro roster.

## 4. Seed 5 listings

Five real posts on that corridor, each with a lat/lng inside it:

- [ ] At least one `watch` (Neighborhood Watch).
- [ ] At least one `event` (Event Security).
- [ ] At least one `defense` (Physical Defense).
- [ ] `teamSize` stays 1 unless that captain already has people who will join.
- [ ] Titles are human labels. No placeholder or faker text.

## 5. Walk the loop once

On a call, with those people:

- [ ] Create a listing.
- [ ] It shows as a pin on `/map`.
- [ ] A provider accepts it (or the captain accepts and adds joiners when `teamSize` is above 1).
- [ ] The requester messages the provider.
- [ ] The job moves to in progress, then done.
- [ ] The listing and Jobs show that provider’s badge chips.

## 6. LinkedIn

One post on the existing org, [safety-net-marketplace](https://www.linkedin.com/company/safety-net-marketplace): a map screenshot and the hire-a-neighbor line. That page is a channel. No ideology wall.
